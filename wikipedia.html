Gianluca Mattia Visconti (Italian pronounciation: [d͡ʒanˈlu.ka visˈkon.ti]; born 1 August 19██) is an Italian businessman who currently serves as the vice president of Visconti, his family's namesake olive oil company. He is also one of the directors of Visconti Real Estate. He is the son of ██████ Visconti and ██████ Visconti.

Early life [ edit ]

Gianluca Mattia Visconti was born in Dolomiten Medical Clinic located in Trentino Alto-Adige, Italy, on 1 August 19██. [1] He is the eldest child[2] of Italian businessman ██████ Visconti and ██████ Visconti (née Chambard).

He spent the majority of his childhood travelling, where he grew up alongside his sister, Clelia. In 20██, his mother passed away from an undisclosed illness.[3]

Education [ edit ]

Visconti attended the Institut Le Rosey in Rolle, Switzerland. Afterwards, he went on to attend Blue Ivy University and received two Bachelor's degrees, one in Business and one in Economics.[4] He then went on to study at London Business School for his MBA.[5]

Career [ edit ]

On completing his studies, he returned to Italy to work in the family company. He started in the operations division and oversaw supply chain and logistics.[6] He later switched to management in the Product Development before becoming a Director of Operations. As of 20██, he is the vice president of Visconti and on the board of directors for Visconti Real Estate.[7] [8]

Personal life [ edit ]

Visconti has been in a relationship with Francesca Lucchesi since 20██.[9]

He openly identifies as bisexual.[10]

He has one full sibling, Clelia Visconti-Chambard[11], two half-siblings[12], Felix Gauthier and Mercer Visconti, and one adopted sibling, Sumi Visconti.[13] He is said to be a distant cousin of Josiane Verany [citation needed].
