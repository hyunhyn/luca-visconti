Reputation
Visconti was once noted by peers to be an impulsive and intense individual who never seemed to be satisfied by the work presented to him. College classmates have stated that he came across as both prideful and insecure. Despite Luca's steady work at the company, many newcomers to his division in Visconti lacked respect for his position due to the clear nepotism. Visconti himself had noted in a previous interview that he "can understand where that kind of thought comes from, but at the end of the day, it's not their opinion that matters. If anything, being underestimated is part of the motivation to prove them wrong."
